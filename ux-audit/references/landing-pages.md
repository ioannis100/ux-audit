# Landing and marketing pages

Load when the target is a homepage, landing page or marketing site. Deep conversion
work → hand off to `cro` / `copywriting`. Numbers tagged [H] are practitioner defaults
(mostly Tim Gabe, @timgabe), not standards: suggest, don't fail.

## Structure

- Section order present: hero → value prop (user's pain/goal) → features (how) →
  proof (logos, numbers, testimonials) → closing CTA → footer (full link set = second nav).
  Missing proof or closing CTA = P2.
- **Five questions above the fold** (Krug): What is this? What can I do here? What do
  they have? Why here and not elsewhere? Where do I start? Plus a 6–8-word tagline by
  the logo saying what it is and how it differs — "best-in-class" fluff fails.
- Each section has: headline naming a pain or outcome · subhead adding a *new* angle
  (not a restatement) · visual showing the product in context · proof where relevant ·
  ≤ 1 primary CTA.
- **Fast-scroll test** per section (2s look): what is it / why does it help / what do I
  do next? Any "no" = finding. Fix the delivery, not the amount of content.
- Hero shows the product or its real output (screenshot, output gallery, a live
  interaction like a prompt box) — not team photos or abstract art.
- Outcomes over specs: "Close tickets 3× faster" beats "real-time Jira integration".
  Spec-only headline = P2.
- **Goldilocks copy**: too little leaves objections (price, setup effort, trust,
  switching) unanswered; too much paralyses. Nothing a scanner must read to get the point.
- Copy length fits its component: card titles ~2–3 words; hero headline ≤ ~3 lines.

## CTAs and conversion

- One primary CTA per page goal (Unbounce, 18k pages: 1 CTA 13.5% · 2 CTAs 11.9% ·
  3 CTAs 10.5%). Same label and colour wherever it repeats.
- Complex or mixed-intent product: at most 1 secondary CTA ("Watch demo"), visually
  quieter (outline/text) — never a second filled button.
- Nav: ≤ 1 filled CTA + ≤ 1 text action ("Sign in"). 3+ nav CTAs = P2 (Hick).
- The primary CTA is the only element in its section using the strongest brand colour.
- **Escape valve** for not-ready visitors: chat, help link, save/subscribe — low
  friction, low visual weight — instead of losing them.
- Context: average SaaS visitor→customer < 4%, top performers ~12%.
- **Choice overload**: plan/tier pickers ≤ 3–4 options with one recommended default;
  ≥ 5 undifferentiated options = P2. A CTA may carry a live count ("See all artists
  (1,250)") when true.

## Layout rhythm [H]

- Desktop: 12-column grid; at 1440 wide ≈ 120–160px margins, ~20–24px gutters; or one
  max-width container (~960–1200px) split into equal 1/2/3-up chunks that align across
  sections. Text widths snap to columns; body text ≤ ~600px wide.
- Type roles: H1 + largest body only in hero and closing CTA; H2 = section headings;
  H3 = subsections; dense sections (logos, testimonials, specs) drop 1–2 steps. Base
  body 18px; long-form reading ~20px. Ratio ~1.25–1.3, rounded values.
- Hierarchy steps down the page: hero heading largest (56–160px), later sections smaller,
  card titles a level below their section heading; gaps and CTA size shrink with it.
- Spacing: section padding ≥ 120–160px vertical on desktop; text↔image ≥ 80–96px; card
  padding ~32px. Inside a block, heading+body sit tight (e.g. 16–24px) and the CTA sits
  ~2× further below (32–48px). Equal gaps everywhere looks undesigned.
- Alternate text and media sides across consecutive feature sections; no 4+ identical
  sections in a row.
- **Wave rhythm**: hold the grid, then break it on purpose 1–3 times (full-bleed
  carousel, logo marquee, centred closing CTA, one element escaping the grid). Never
  broken = monotonous; broken everywhere = chaos.
- Nav: logo / centred links / CTA with left and right groups of equal width, so the
  links are truly centred (measure the offset).
- Footer: bold category headings, muted links, columns top-aligned.
- Button size ladder: L 64h / 32px side padding · M 48 / 24 · S 40 / 16. Nav = M; hero
  and closing CTA = L. Same role → same size everywhere.

## Assets and coherence

- Assets fill > 50% of a landing layout (assets + text ≈ 80–90%): low-res, stretched,
  mismatched or generic stock visuals = P2 first-impression finding.
- One visual language: no photos mixed into flat illustrations; one illustration
  library; one stroke-based icon set; backgrounds removed/consistent.
- Logo bar: logos at equal visual size, one colour (mono/grey).
- Brand colour relates to the assets (CTA colour sampled from hero imagery, or imagery
  recoloured to the palette). Base colour derives from the product/brand.
- Text on images: a localized radial/centre scrim to pass 4.5:1, not a heavy full-bleed
  dark gradient.
- Coherence: the hero image echoed later (blurred, low-opacity "aurora" backgrounds);
  empty corners filled with on-style elements, never a new style.
- Template tells: stock radii, fonts, placeholder sections and imagery left unchanged.
  Fix levers: radii, texture/pattern, brand fonts, custom copy, custom imagery, delete
  unused sections.
- Check both 1440 and ~375–390 widths for every section.

## Product and pricing pages [H]

- Status badge above the title (Best-seller / New / Top-rated) when true.
- Product shown in use, not isolated on white.
- Specific, non-round proof ("4.9 · 221 reviews", "500+ sold this week") — real numbers
  only (emotional.md → ethics gate).
- Options as visible swatches with a description at the hesitation point (hover/tap).
- Plan cards side by side; the recommended one tinted + "Most popular" + reassurance
  inside (save %, cancel anytime). Pre-selection only when it is genuinely best value
  and the alternative is equally visible.
- Trust badges answer the audience's specific fears (third-party tested, 60-day
  guarantee), not generic expectations (free shipping).
- Reframe cost as convenience only where true: ETA or time saved next to the price;
  add-ons relative to the main purchase ("2.6% of your order").
