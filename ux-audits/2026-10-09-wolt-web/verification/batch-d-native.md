## Corrections to F-NATIVE-01 (beyond severity)
- The basket never says "applied" or "unlocked": the green pill reads "5 € off on orders over 12 €" under "Unlock savings". The user's inference is the problem; quote the copy exactly.
- "Pick one" is **not** a choice between stacking savings. The group holds only the €5 offer and "Do not apply offers"; the 0 € delivery deal is applied separately. So the default is a plain opt-in. The likely rationale is that this is a one-time new-user promo users may want to save for later. It is defensible as a choice, but not after a celebration.
- The offer terms (`v02`) also say "For new users with a local phone number", "Valid for 14 days from registration" and "Cannot be combined with other promotions". Yet a guest sees the unlock, and once selected it **stacks** with the 0 € delivery deal (−7,65 €). Either the terms or the checkout is wrong. A signed-in user may lose it at pay time; that is unverified (pay is behind sign-in).
- Better fix: at unlock, say "Unlocked — select it at checkout to save 5 €" (or pre-select it with an easy untick). Auto-applying a one-time promo to the first eligible small order may not be what the user wants.
