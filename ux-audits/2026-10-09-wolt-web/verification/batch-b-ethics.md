## Black recommendations (black-ux.md §2 gate, as written)
B-01 resume card with the real closing time | **PASSES, with two required amendments** | (1) User's goal ✓: it is the user's own basket. (2) True: conditional. The mechanism is truthful where I reproduced it (McDonald's: header = toast = hours). But ED's "verified: closing times match" has no artifact behind it, and it conflicts with the 03:30 seen twice at Delulu. Also, "closes at" is not the order cutoff: the toast says "Your order may not be accepted" before closing. The card must show the **last-order time**, or it overstates the time left. (3) Easy out: "Clear" is one tap with no penalty copy, so it passes the letter, but the only way to turn the card off deletes the basket. Add a non-destructive "×" (hide the card, keep the basket). (4) Vulnerable ✓: off for alcohol and Adults Only baskets, never pushed to guests. (5) Guardrail ✓: clear rate plus refunds and cancellations after 00:00.
B-02 true per-item order counts | **FAILS (item 3), and the guardrail is mismatched** | (1) ✓ It helps choose a dish. (2) True only as a promise: ED couldn't verify what "Popular" is based on. The data likely exists, since venues already have a "Most ordered" section. (3) **Easy out ✗**: a count printed on an item card can't be snoozed, hidden or turned off. ED replaced the gate's test with "no pressure copy". (4) ✓ Excluded from alcohol and Adults Only items. (5) Weak: dish-level refund rate doesn't measure the stated harm (herding, burying new dishes). Use new-dish trial share, menu diversity and complaints. Fix: a true, calm count is ordinary information, not a black technique. Move it to a regular recommendation with the rule "real order data, no pressure copy", or drop it from the Black tier.
